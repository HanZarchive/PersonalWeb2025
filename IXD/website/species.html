Wildlife groups observed across U.S. National Parks

Mammals

2 groups
Large Mammals
Peak Activity Autumn
Includes elk, black bear, bison, mountain lion, moose, and wolf. Most active during dawn and dusk.
Small Mammals
High Activity Spring
Includes squirrels, raccoons, river otters, beavers, and marmots. Often spotted near water and forest edges.

Birds

3 groups
Raptors
Medium Activity Winter
Eagles, hawks, falcons, and owls. Hunt in open meadows, cliff edges, and along river corridors.
Waterbirds
High Activity Autumn
Herons, egrets, Canada geese, mergansers, and loons. Concentrated near lakes, rivers, and wetlands.
Songbirds & Others
Peak Activity Spring
Mountain bluebirds, warblers, finches, thrushes, and woodpeckers. Peak during spring migration.

Reptiles & Amphibians

2 groups
Reptiles
Medium Activity Summer
Rattlesnakes, garter snakes, lizards, and desert tortoises. Activity peaks in warm dry months.
Amphibians
Peak Activity Summer
Pacific tree frogs, spotted salamanders, tiger salamanders, and toads. Most active near water after rains.

Insects & Invertebrates

2 groups
Butterflies & Moths
Medium Activity Summer
Monarch, swallowtail, and fritillary butterflies; luna and sphinx moths. Visible in meadows and wildflower zones.
Other Insects
Low Activity Summer
Dragonflies, beetles, grasshoppers, and fireflies. Found in diverse habitats from ponds to grasslands.

Fish & Aquatic Life

2 groups
Fish
High Activity Summer
Sockeye salmon, rainbow trout, cutthroat trout, and steelhead. Tracked in rivers, lakes, and coastal streams.
Marine Mammals
Medium Activity Spring
River otters, mink, and aquatic-adapted species near park waterways. Best spotted at dawn along riverbanks.
