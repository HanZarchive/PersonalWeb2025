Counting Rhythm Machine

Individual Work · October 2025

# Can machines count what they cannot feel?

This visual sequence explores the boundary between human and machine perception of rhythm. While humans count beats for resonance and joy, machines count for precision—until the rhythm refuses to be counted. As the music evolves from regular to irregular, watch the machine's confidence dissolve. What humans hear as beauty, the machine reads as error.
